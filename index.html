Happy anniversary, my loves, my aesthley!

I take back what I said last time na the first gift gyud kay paper flower, because now that I think about it, the actual first gift was you coming into my life. I never really expected someone like you to suddenly walk into my life, much less someone who would actually like me. Honestly, I never thought you'd see something in me because I always saw myself as just a mediocre person. But somehow, here we are, and I couldn't be any more grateful.

Pero regardless, this isn't really about me. I just want to tell you, from the bottom of my heart, that you are one of the greatest people in my life. You have become such a big part of who I am as a person, and I hope you know how much you mean to me. I hope that as we continue this journey together, we get to mold each other into better versions of ourselves. I want us to grow, achieve our dreams, become successful, and celebrate every little victory together.

I know college hasn't been easy for you, especially adjusting to this new environment, but always remember that your boyfriend will be here for you through it all, okay? Don't pressure yourself too much because life isn't just about school. I hope you still find time to enjoy life, express yourself, and embrace that freedom and creativity that I adore so much about you. Never lose that part of yourself, my love.

I really, really hope to be with you forever and ever. I want us to build the greatest story of our lives, one filled with memories, stupid jokes, random adventures, little arguments that we eventually laugh about, and countless moments where we get to remind each other how much we love one another. I don't expect everything to be perfect, but I do hope that whatever life throws at us, we continue choosing each other.

And oh my days, I really love Claude for assisting me with this whole website thingy HAHAHAHA. But I swear, dugay kaayo ko sa part sa pagsulat ani because I wanted to be careful with every word. I didn't want to just throw random sweet words at you. I wanted to write something that genuinely came from me, something that I could read back and hopefully makahilak ko while writing it huhuhuha ewdawdad. You deserve that much effort, and honestly, even this little letter doesn't feel enough to express everything I have for you.

I love you so, so much, my baby, my aesthley, my everything. I hope everything you've ever wished for and everything you're working so hard to achieve will eventually come into fruition (thank you merriam-webster sa definition of fruition). I hope life becomes kinder to you, that you find happiness in the little things, and that you never run out of reasons to smile. I'll always be cheering for you, whether you're winning or struggling, because I'll be proud of you either way.

Happy anniversary again, my love. Thank you for being my first gift, my favorite person, and one of the most beautiful things that ever happened to me.

Here's to us, to growing together, and to forever.

I LOVE YOU SO MUCHHHHH. MWA MWA MWA MWA MWA.

Forever yours,
Ramitan
